Start in the yard, not the attic.

A rat in the ceiling got there from somewhere, and the route almost always begins outdoors. Roof rats favor anything elevated: oak limbs that brush shingles, power lines, fence rails, palm crowns and the tops of sheds. Norway rats stay near the ground and dig along foundations, paving edges and the base of fences.

Live oaks and their draped moss shade many Winter Park lots, and a mature canopy can hand an animal a ready-made bridge from tree to eave. Palms add dead fronds that hold shelter and fruit that holds food. None of that is a defect, only a set of routes worth reading before a trap is chosen.

What the first lap covers.

  • Limbs, vines and palm fronds within a few feet of the roof edge.
  • Soffit panels, ridge vents and the gaps where cables or pipes enter the wall.
  • Fallen fruit, feeder seed, pet food and compost on the ground.
  • Burrow holes along the slab, fence line or shed foundation.
  • Grease smears and droppings on beams, wires and fence tops.

Those five checks usually reveal which species we are dealing with. A shiny rub mark on a wire above the garage points up, and a fresh burrow under a shed points down. The plan differs for each.

Trapping and what follows it.

Rats distrust unfamiliar objects, so stations may sit unarmed for a few days before they are set. Stations go on the travel lanes, tucked against walls and out of reach of children and pets. We log each location so the revisit starts from the same map.

Catching rats without closing the way in only resets the clock. Once the lanes are known, the exclusion crew caps soffits, vents and wall penetrations with metal, and cleanup waits until nothing is moving. If insulation is fouled, the attic cleanup page explains the next step.

Wooded edges and garden borders.

Mead Botanical Garden is a Winter Park park that, according to Wikipedia, takes in several ecosystems, and some callers describe their street as near it. The garden itself is not our concern. What matters at your address is whether the lot meets a wooded border, a lake shore or a neighbor's overgrown fence, because each one lengthens the approach to your roofline. Tell the dispatcher what touches your property line and we plan the first lap around it.

Timing the work.

Roof rats do not hibernate, and warm weather keeps them active through the winter months. That makes calendar timing less useful than condition timing: after a storm drops a limb on your roof, after fruit drops, after a vent cover gets knocked loose. If any of those happened recently, mention it when you call.

Do this today.

  • Carry grass seed, dog food and feeder mix indoors until this is sorted..
  • Bag any gnawed item and save it for the visit..

Before you call, a few rules to protect yourself.

This box repeats guidance from public health agencies, and it fits any household whether or not a technician is ever booked.

  • Skip the broom and the shop vacuum. Dry droppings crumble, and anything that stirs them sends fine particles into your breathing space.
  • Thirty minutes of airing comes before any cleanup in an enclosed area, according to the CDC.
  • Bare skin should never meet pellets, urine spots or nesting scraps, so gloves go on first.
  • Wipe up wet waste with paper towels and never dry cloth, and keep soaking until nothing looks dusty.
  • Dead animals stay off bare hands, and a gloved hand or a shovel moves them into the bag.
  • Pest work is our limit, and if you feel ill after touching rodent waste, a clinician is the right call.

Rat questions from central Florida callers.

Roof rat or Norway rat: how can I tell?

Roof rats are slimmer with a long tail and travel overhead. Norway rats are heavier and stay low, tunneling beside slabs and sheds. Droppings and gnaw marks help us decide.

Will trimming my oak keep rats away?

A limb resting on the shingles acts as a footbridge, so trimming it closes one route. It does not close the gaps in your soffit, so both jobs matter.

Are rats in the attic dangerous to clean up?

Dust from dry droppings is the concern. Follow the CDC steps in the box on this page, and leave heavy cleanup to a technician.

Do palms attract rats?

Dead fronds and fruit clusters give roof rats shelter and food. Removing them near the house shortens the path to your eaves.

Can you come out the same day?

When the schedule has space, yes. The line is staffed all night, so describe what you heard and ask.
