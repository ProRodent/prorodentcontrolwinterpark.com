Coverage questions.

Why is there no page for Mid Florida?

Mid Florida appears in postal data for ZIP codes 32745 and 32799, but we could not confirm it as a real place, so we do not list it as one. Those codes also fall outside the range we serve.

What about ZIP codes just past the limit?

A few sit slightly beyond fifteen miles, such as 32831 and 32821 in Orlando and 32766 in Oviedo. They are not covered, and the dispatcher can say whether a special trip is possible.

My ZIP code is listed but I am near the edge. Is that fine?

If the ZIP code is on the list we cover it. Distances are measured to the middle of the ZIP area, so edge addresses are judged by phone.

Is the Brewer Avenue address a shop?

No. It is a citation address used for listings, and nobody staffs it. Visits take place at your property, and booking is by phone.
