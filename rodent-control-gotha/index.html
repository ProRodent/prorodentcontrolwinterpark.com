Storms, heat and rodent movement.

Humidity matters because it keeps stored goods and insulation damp, and damp material is easier to chew and nest in. Dry out what you can and store the rest in sealed bins.

One ZIP code, many kinds of property.

A place with a single ZIP code can hold houses, sheds, barns and workshops on the same road. Describe what you have, including any outbuilding, and tell us which one the signs appeared in.

If the signs are in an outbuilding, mention its floor, whether it is slab, wood or dirt, and what is stored inside, since feed, firewood and cardboard all change the plan.
