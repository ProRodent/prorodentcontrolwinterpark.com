Information we receive.

This site has no forms and no sign-up boxes, so it does not collect your name or email through the pages themselves. When you phone or email, we receive what you decide to tell us, for example your address, callback number and the nature of the trouble.

How we use it.

We use contact details to book, perform and follow up on service, and to answer your questions. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and analytics.

The pages are static and do not set cookies of their own. The embedded map and the web fonts are loaded from third parties, who may receive technical data such as your IP address under their own policies.
