What changes across a county line.

Nothing about mice or rats respects a county boundary. What differs is the paperwork, such as which permit office handles a repair that needs one. We state the county on this page so you know which jurisdiction we mean.

If a repair involves structural work, ask your city office whether a permit applies. We handle pest work and sealing, and we flag anything that belongs with a licensed contractor.

Inspect, trap, seal, recheck.

Expect a walk around the property, a look at the attic or crawl space, a written list of what was found and a plan in order. Nobody should start sealing before the trapping plan is clear. Anything already gnawed gets mended afterward, as covered under damage repair.

What gets reported first.

None of the clues below is proof by itself. Two or three together usually are.

  • Droppings on the attic insulation.
  • Noises inside a wall after the house goes quiet.
  • A hole under the sink where a pipe enters.
  • A pet that stares at one spot in the wall.

Does this sound like your house? Describe the signs aloud and a staffer tells you what they probably mean.

Shade trees and attic access.

Compost heaps and unsecured garbage cans are among the more common food sources around homes. Use lidded containers and keep them a bit away from the house walls.

Heat, storms and shifting burrows.

Central Florida has no real cold season to shut rodents down. Warm, humid air keeps mice and rats moving year-round, and summer storms flood burrows and push animals toward dry shelter, sometimes your garage or attic.
