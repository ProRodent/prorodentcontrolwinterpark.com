Before you call: what the CDC asks of any household.

Thinking of tidying up before our crew arrives? Work through this list in order and stop at any point that feels unclear.

  • Pellets that look harmless on a shelf release grit when scuffed, which is why dry cleanup is discouraged.
  • Fresh air does the first part of the job: half an hour with the openings wide and nobody in the room.
  • Peel the gloves off inside out when you are done, bag them if they are disposable and scrub with soap and water.
  • Spray droppings and urine spots with disinfectant, or with bleach cut with water, until they glisten, then allow roughly five minutes.
  • One tied bag per cleanup session keeps things simple and keeps the waste contained.
  • Tell your doctor about any rodent contact if you fall ill within a few weeks, since we stick to pest control.
