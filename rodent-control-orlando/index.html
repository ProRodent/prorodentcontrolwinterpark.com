Before you call: federal health guidance in short form.

Thinking of tidying up before our crew arrives? Work through this list in order and stop at any point that feels unclear.

  • Skip the broom and the shop vacuum. Dry droppings crumble, and anything that stirs them sends fine particles into your breathing space.
  • Garages, sheds, attics and spare rooms that stayed shut tend to hold stale air, so open them and wait about thirty minutes.
  • Disposable gloves suit a one-time cleanup, while thick rubber ones suit bigger jobs and can be washed afterward.
  • The CDC bleach recipe is one part bleach to nine parts water, mixed fresh for the job.
  • Everything touched by the cleanup, from towels to gloves, rides to the outdoor bin in a knotted bag.
  • Feeling sick after handling rodent waste is a matter for a physician, so tell them exactly what you touched.
