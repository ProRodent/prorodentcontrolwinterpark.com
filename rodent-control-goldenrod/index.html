Goldenrod, a place on the county line.

Goldenrod has no city government, because it is a census-designated place that lies partly in Orange County and partly in Seminole County. One ZIP code represents it in our data, 32733, about 5.9 miles from Winter Park. We use such facts only to orient ourselves. The traps, the sealing and the inspection depend on your building.

A census-designated place is a statistical area. That label does not change the work, but it does explain why the address on a bill or letter may name another town. Give the dispatcher the street and the 32733 ZIP code and the rest follows.

A year of rodent weather.

Wet weather rearranges rodent habits more than it removes them. Flooded burrows send animals looking for dry ground, and a gap in a wall can look like a good place to wait out the rain.

Put the broom down before dialing.

Thinking of tidying up before our crew arrives? Work through this list in order and stop at any point that feels unclear.

  • Put your energy into wetting things down and not into sweeping them up, because wet first is the whole point of this step.
  • Before cleanup, crack every opening in the space and busy yourself elsewhere for half an hour.
  • Peel the gloves off inside out when you are done, bag them if they are disposable and scrub with soap and water.
  • Wipe up wet waste with paper towels and never dry cloth, and keep soaking until nothing looks dusty.
  • Throw the bag away with ordinary garbage and rinse any reusable tools you handled.
  • Feeling sick after handling rodent waste is a matter for a physician, so tell them exactly what you touched.
